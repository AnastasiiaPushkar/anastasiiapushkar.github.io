About

Hi, I'm Anastasiia

I'm a brand designer and illustrator from Kyiv. For five years I've been designing logos, identity systems and the materials around them, from flyers to product visuals, and for the last two I've worked with brands from the US, Europe, New Zealand and India.

Before any visuals I look at who the brand is for and how it should feel. Then I explore a few directions and build a system with its own logic and character. Years of art school and oil painting still shape how I see colour and composition.

    From a pencil line to a system

    A logo drawn by hand carries a little imperfection that type alone can't give. I sketch on paper, draw custom letters by hand in Procreate, then rebuild everything in vector so it works at every size and on every surface.

    1. 1Sketch on paper
